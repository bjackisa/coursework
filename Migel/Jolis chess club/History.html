History of chess;

Chess originated in India around the 6th century AD as chaturanga,
a game representing military units—infantry, cavalry, elephants, and chariots.
It traveled to Persia (becoming shatranj), then to the Islamic world, and finally into Europe by 1000 AD.
Modern rules and faster gameplay emerged around 1500 in Spain and Italy.
