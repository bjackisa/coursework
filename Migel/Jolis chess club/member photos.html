Father Gramies Games

Over Two hundred thirty school chess teams from 186 primary and secondary schools across the country gathered at Ntare School in Mbarara City to participate in the 36th edition of the Father Grimes National Schools’ Chess Championship. The year’s edition, themed “Chess for Financial Inclusion,” is jointly sponsored by Bank of Uganda, Bank of Baroda, and Diamond Trust Bank. Winners received solar panels, trophies, medals, and certificates.
